Past Performance

This chart shows the share class’s performance as the percentage loss or gain per year over the last 10 years against its benchmark.

Please be aware that past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

The past performance is shown after deduction of ongoing charges. Any entry/exit fees are excluded from the calculation.

Past performance has been calculated in SGD and is expressed as a percentage change of the Portfolio's net asset value at each year end.

The benchmark performance is in a different currency than the share class which may impact the performance comparison.

The past performance prior to 2022 is calculated from the past performance of Class A2 USD converted at the applicable spot exchange rate.

Performance is shown only where full calendar-year performance history is available.
