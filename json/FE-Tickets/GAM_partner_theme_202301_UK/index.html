What is this product?

Type

The Product is a share of the Sub-fund Pragma Global (the sub-fund) which is part of the Multibrand SICAV-SIF (the "Umbrella Fund"), a “société d'investissement à capital variable” (SICAV) and authorised as an undertaking for collective investments (UCI) under Part II of the law dated 17 December 2010 on UCIs (the “2010 Law”). The Company qualifies as an alternative investment fund (“AIF”) under the Law of July 12, 2013, that implements the EU Directive 2011/61/EU on Alternative Investment Fund Managers (“AIFM Directive”) into Luxembourg law (the “AIFM Law”).

Term

The fund has no maturity date. The Fund could be closed under the conditions led down in the current prospectus of the Fund.

Objectives

The PRAGMA GLOBAL focuses on capital preservation and consistent long term capital appreciation. It is believed that long term capital appreciation may be achieved by delivering positive compounded growth throughout all market cycles. The Subfund may have flexible investment strategies with absolute return objectives, allow for the use of derivatives, leverage and short selling and is expected to generate returns that have low correlation with traditional asset classes. These investment strategies will be pursued by redominantly investing in underlying funds ("Target Funds") with a proven, solid track record. It is envisaged that the "longshort equity" Target Fund strategy will constitute the core of the Subfund. Additionally, the investment strategy will be enhanced by the use of ETFs or "smart Beta" strategies on a selective basis and in conjunction with the top down view of the investment manager.

Intended Retail Investors

The fund is aimed at professional clients and eligible counterparties and may also be suitable for private clients, who intend general capital preservation and have a long-term investment horizon. This fund may be for investors with advanced knowledge or experience with financial products. The investor can bear financial losses and attaches no importance to capital guarantees.

Depositary

The custodian is State Street Bank International GmbH, Luxembourg Branch, 49, Avenue J.F. Kennedy, L-1855 Luxemburg.

Investment performance information

The Fund focuses on capital preservation and consistent long term capital appreciation. It is believed that long term capital appreciation may be achieved by delivering positive compounded growth throughout all market cycles. The performance of the Fund and the returns may be affected by market volatility, political and economic risks which could also have impact on the interest rates, credit risks and exchange rates.

The Reference Index used for the calculation of the Hurdle Rate is SOFR 1 Month (Secured Overnight Financing Rate).

What could affect my return positively?

The Fund may have flexible investment strategies with absolute return objectives to generate returns that have low correlation with traditional asset classes. However, it's envisaged that the “long-short equity” target fund strategy will constitute the core of the subfund. Therefore, the return of the fund will be affected positively mainly by the positive performance of this core strategy.

What could affect my return negatively?

It is believed that long term capital appreciation may be achieved by delivering positive compounded growth throughout all market cycles. Still the Fund is likely to perform adversely during periods of low or negative economic growth, high and unstable inflation and high unemployment. Changes in tax, regulation or law can also impact returns negatively.

During severe adverse market conditions, it may take longer than usual to redeem units in the PRIIP. In order to redeem units, assets may have to be sold and/or could become illiquid.

What happens if GAM (Luxembourg) S.A. is unable to pay out?

For your protection the company's assets are held with a separate company, a depositary, so the fund's ability to pay out would not be affected by the insolvency of the Fund Management Company. If the fund is terminated or wound up, the assets will be liquidated and you will receive an appropriate share of any proceeds but you may lose part or all of your investment.
