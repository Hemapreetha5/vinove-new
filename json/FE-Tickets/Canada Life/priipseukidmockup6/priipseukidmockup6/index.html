What is this Fund?

A unit-linked internal collective life insurance fund, the performance of which is linked to the investment as identified above. A unit-linked insured fund is a pooled investment that allows you to combine your money with that of other customers. Fund managers will then invest this money in underlying assets in line with the objectives of the fund.

The fund aims to provide a return by way of income with some capital growth.

The intended retail investor is outlined in the Key Investor Document for the CanInvest Select Account through which this fund can be accessed.

What are the risks and what could I get in return?

The summary risk indicator (SRI) is a guide to the level of risk of this product compared to other products. It shows how likely it is that the product will lose money because of movements in the market or because we are not able to pay you.

Lower riskHigher risk

In simple terms, the lower the SRI, the lower the assessed risk of the product, and also the lower the assessed potential future returns of the product. Likewise, the higher the SRI, the higher the assessed risk of the product, and also the higher the assessed potential future returns. However, the assigned SRI should not be seen as a guarantee of actual risk or actual future returns, and instead should only to be used as a guide to understanding the potential risks and rewards of a fund in as simple terms as possible.

This fund does not include any protection from future market performance so you could lose some, or all, of your investment. If we are not able to pay you what is owed, you could lose your entire investment. However, you may benefit from a consumer protection scheme (see the section “What happens if Canada Life is unable to pay out?” in the Key Information Document). The indicator shown above and below does not consider this protection.

This fund is rated as 2 out of 7 which is a low risk class. This rates the potential losses from future performance at a low level, and poor market conditions are very unlikely to impact the amount you could get back.

Investment performance information

This product invests directly into a collective investment scheme (CIS) which invests mainly in the wider asset class of: Mixed Asset (MXAS), and invests mainly in the geographical area of: Global (GLOB). Therefore any growth or decline in any of these markets will likely have an impact on the performance of this product. The product is considered to be in the investment sector of: IA Mixed Investment 0-35% Shares, and is aligned to the benchmark of: IA Mixed Investment 0%-35% Shares.

What could affect my return positively?

DUMMY INFORMATION - WHAT COULD AFFECT MY RETURNS POSITIVELY

Any affect on the total returns on the product, whether positive or negative, can be caused by many different factors.

This product invests directly into a collective investment scheme (CIS), and as such, the CIS will hold a number of different assets within itself. Ultimately it is the sum total of the collective positive and negative affects on these individual assets which will amount to the total returns on this particular product.

Price Risk or Market Value Risk: The risk of price fluctuations impacting the investments held.
Interest Rate Risk: The risk of varying interest rates impacting the investments held.
Currency Risk or Foreign Exchange Risk: The risk of varying exchange rates impacting the investments held.
Country Risk: The product invests in a CIS which may hold investments in different countries. All countries carry their own unique risks, including interest rates, exchange rates, political ideologies, and economy types.
Liquidity Risk: In unfavourable market conditions, assets may be harder to sell in a timely manner and at reasonable prices. This could have a negative impact on the value of the product and could affect the ability to meet investors’ redemptions.
Counterparty or Credit Risk: The risk that any firm involved in a transaction may expose the product to financial loss. Where an underlying investment is impacted by a change to its credit rating, that investment may become more or less attractive, which could cause the price to rise or fall.
