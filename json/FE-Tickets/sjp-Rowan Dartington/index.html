Investment Objective & Risk

This portfolio is suited to those investors who wish to take higher levels of risk in the expectation of higher returns in the future. In order to potentially capture higher levels of returns the portfolio will invest almost exclusively in funds invested in equities and look to the potential of smaller companies that have greater growth prospects than large companies. The choice of funds will be based around a robust investment framework to ensure there is a balance and diversification to the model. There will be high levels of overseas exposure and with the potentially higher return nature of these investments, volatility will be higher also.
