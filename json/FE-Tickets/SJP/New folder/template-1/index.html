Assessing Performance

-

Macro diversified growth: these strategies identify investment opportunities based on the manager’s macroeconomic views. They can invest in a broad range of asset classes and regions.

Macro bond: these strategies identify investment opportunities based on the manager’s macroeconomic views, with a focus on fixed income markets and currency. They aim to generate returns by taking views on changes in inflation, interest rates, credit and FX environments.

Absolute return bond: these strategies invest in fixed income, aiming to deliver a positive return over the longer term while also protecting capital. These strategies seek investment opportunities across the fixed income universe and will generally have lower exposure to changes in interest rates.

Equity long/short: these strategies invest in equities using both long positions (a purchase of a stock) and short positions (a sale of a stock, with the expectation that it will decrease in value). These strategies source returns from the manager’s stock selection skills or investment style. These strategies can have some tilt towards market returns or can be neutral to the wider equity market, but returns are typically generated independently from directional equity market movements.
