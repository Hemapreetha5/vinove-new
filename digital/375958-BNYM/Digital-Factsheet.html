Historic Yield is intended for use primarily by equity funds and multi asset funds. It reflects distributions declared over the past twelve months as a percentage of the mid-market unit price, as at the date shown. Distribution Yield and Underlying Yield are both used by bond funds. The Underlying Yield reflects the annualised income net of expenses of the fund (calculated in accordance with relevant accounting standards) as percentage of the mid-market unit price of the fund as at the date shown. Fund Characteristic
The Fund follows a premium income approach to global equity investment, applying strict yield-based investment criteria. The Fund emphasises shares in cash-generative companies with attractive and sustainable dividend yields, through a constantly evolving and forward-looking approach that anticipates change and identifies opportunities.

Reasons to Invest
The case for equity income: The defensive nature of dividend-paying companies can create an asymmetric return profile.
