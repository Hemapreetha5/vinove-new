Fund objective & investment strategy
The fund aims to achieve capital appreciation over the medium to long term by investing in UK equities. Investment may be across any or all economic sectors. Returns are linked to both the UK and global economy and the market supply and demand of shares based on expectations of future profitability.

Appropriateness
This fund is appropriate for investors who are willing to take a calculated risk for the prospect of more attractive returns. Investors in this fund are comfortable with investing in the stock markets and are aware that there may be some fluctuations and volatility of returns and that the value of your investments can go down as well as up.
