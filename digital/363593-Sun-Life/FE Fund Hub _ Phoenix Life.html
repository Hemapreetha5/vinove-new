Guide to how we work out unit-linked prices

Important information about fund prices

  • Prices are shown in pence (Sterling). For example, 101 means £1.01.
  • The offer price is the price you pay for each unit.
  • The bid price is the price you get when you sell each unit. If you switch between funds, you will normally sell units at the bid price in the first fund and buy units at the bid price in the second.
  • The price of units can go down as well as up and is not guaranteed. However, there are a number of cash based funds where Phoenix Life is currently guaranteeing that the bid price will not fall.
  • Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future performance.
  • To view a fund factsheet, please click on the name of the fund. If the factsheet for your fund is not available, please send us an email.
  • If you invest in a with-profits fund, please visit the with-profits section.

Janus Henderson Investors (JHI) announced that they are closing the Janus Henderson UK Gilt Fund Z ACC (GB00B03TPF38) and the Janus Henderson Index Linked Bond Fund Z ACC (GB00B03TPR59) on the 14th March 2024.

For unit linked funds that invest in these underlying assets, they will instead be invested in the PUTM Bothwell Sterling Government Bond Fund B Acc (GB00B23YX143) and the PUTM Bothwell Sterling Index Linked Fund B Acc (GB00B52QR215). These are two funds provided by Phoenix Unit Trust Managers Limited (PUTM), a member of the Phoenix Group.

Note that whilst the JHI and PUTM gilt funds have the same index benchmark (the FTSE Actuaries UK Conventional Gilts All Stocks Index), the closing JHI Index Linked fund was benchmarked against the FTSE Actuaries UK Index-Linked Gilts greater than 5 years to maturity Index, whilst the replacement PUTM index linked fund is currently benchmarked against the FTSE UK Index-Linked Gilts All Stocks Index.
