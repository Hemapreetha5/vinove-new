Fund Characteristic
在新興市場資產類別中，企業債券現正急速發展。本基金投資於在新興市場上市或成立的企業所發行的債券及類似債務工具。作為本基金的管理公司，Insight設有資深的新興市場投資專家團隊，自1998年以來一直主管新興市場債券投資賬戶。專家團隊成員背靠專業交易員及整體固定收益投資團隊的龐大支援。基金可投資於新興市場、高信貸評級債券、衍生工具、貨幣及貨幣相關投資。

推薦理由
A systematic process, aiming to generate consistent outperformance: The Fund takes a total return approach, dynamically allocating across the emerging markets corporate high yield and investment grade spectrum, with active management through the interest rate and credit cycles. Securities are selected utilising Insight’s rigorous credit research process, drawing upon fundamental country evaluation to focus on those companies underpinned by stable or improving economic conditions in their core business markets.
Rigorous approach to risk management: Insight has developed a proprietary risk framework specifically for its emerging markets portfolios, with internal risk limits applied at both the individual country and the aggregate portfolio level. Mitigating top-down risk is a core aspect of Insight’s approach and the team generally avoids investing in countries with an economically regressive and deteriorating political outlook.
Dedicated and highly experienced team: The Fund is managed by a highly experienced team of emerging markets investment specialists who are supported by dedicated traders and Insight’s broader Fixed Income Group. The team has been managing emerging markets corporate bonds as part of global and wider emerging markets bond mandates since 2005.
