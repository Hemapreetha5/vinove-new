Fund Characteristic
基金投資於一系列不同規模的企業，不設行業限制。一般而言，在投資組合中，基金集中從新興及成熟亞太區（日本除外）市場納入不多於70隻精選股票，並不受參考指數約束。此類股票具備增長潛力，估值處於合理水平。研究及選股方針乃建基於Newton獨有主題投資策略。

推薦理由
Exposure to diverse markets in a promising region: Invested in companies in the Asia Pacific ex Japan region, the Fund offers investors exposure to the dynamic growth of the region as well as the stability or familiarity of more developed markets.
Focused on the future of Asia: The Fund seeks to invest in Asian companies with long growth trajectories that generate real value from their growth and reinvest it for the benefit of all shareholders. The portfolio is focused on the future of Asia, rather than old profit pools.
Experienced Asian equity team: Deep-seated research is conducted by a specialist team and highly experienced career analysts, with integrated corporate governance scrutiny.
