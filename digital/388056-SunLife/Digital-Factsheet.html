Fund objective & investment strategy
The fund aims to achieve capital appreciation over the medium to long term by investing in commercial property. Returns are based on property values and rental income.

Appropriateness
This fund is appropriate for investors who are willing to accept moderate levels of risk for the prospect of slightly higher returns than money deposits and may be wary of investing in the stock market. Investors in this fund accept that in exceptional circumstances, there may be a delay in cashing in or switching your units as properties take time to sell.

Potential risks
Past performance should not be taken as a guide to future performance; the value of your investments, and any income received, can go down as well as up and you may not get back the amount of your original investment. Inflation, over time, will erode the buying power of your investment.
Funds with investments in property carry specific risks. The value of property is generally a matter of a valuer’s opinion rather than fact and there may also be challenges when it comes to selling property. Funds that invest in money market instruments may be affected if an institution which they invest in experiences financial difficulty.
