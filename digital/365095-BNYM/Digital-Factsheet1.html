Investment Objective
To achieve long term capital growth through investment primarily (meaning at least two-thirds of the Fund's assets) in a portfolio of equity and up to one-third of the Sub-Fund's assets in a portfolio of equity-related securities of companies located in Asia (excluding Japan) or deriving a preponderant part of their income therefrom.
Fund Description
The Fund seeks to generate capital growth, as well as a sustainable and attractive income, over the long-term through a high-conviction portfolio of Asia Pacific ex Japan equities.

Fund Characteristic
The Fund offers investors access to a concentrated portfolio invested in businesses which the investment team believes stand to benefit from the future growth of the Asian region. The investment approach is conviction-based with no sector constraints, and invests primarily in the Asia-Pacific region, excluding Japan. A constantly evolving and forward-looking approach seeks to anticipate change, manage risk and identify opportunities.
