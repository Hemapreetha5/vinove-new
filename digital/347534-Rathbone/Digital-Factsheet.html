The objective of the fund is to deliver a greater total return than the Bank of England’s Base Rate + 2%, after fees, over any three-year period. The fund aims to deliver this return with no more than one-third of the volatility of the FTSE Developed stock market Index. There is no guarantee that this investment objective will be achieved over three years, or any other time period. Your capital is at risk. We aim to deliver this return with no more than one-third of the volatility of the FTSE Developed stock market Index.
