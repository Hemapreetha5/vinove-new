Historic Yield is intended for use primarily by equity funds and multi asset funds. It reflects distributions declared over the past twelve months as a percentage of the mid-market unit price, as at the date shown. It does not include any preliminary charge and investors may be subject to tax on their distributions.
Distribution Yield and Underlying Yield are both used by bond funds. The Distribution Yield reflects the income that may be expected to be distributed, over the next twelve months as a percentage of the mid-market unit price of the fund as at the date shown. The Underlying Yield reflects the annualised income net of expenses of the fund (calculated in accordance with relevant accounting standards) as percentage of the mid-market unit price of the fund as at the date shown. Both are based on a snapshot of the portfolio on that day. They do not include any preliminary charge and investors may be subject to tax on distributions.

Team

At Newton, analysis is a career path, not a stepping stone; many of the firm’s experienced analysts have worked directly in the sectors for which they are responsible.

Philosophy
Newton is committed to achieving its clients’ investment objectives. It has a robust investment approach which combines rigorous fundamental analysis with the broad perspective of its global investment themes. Its themes represent its convictions about long-term forces of change across global markets and industries, helping it to focus on long-term opportunities and risks, and shielding out short-term market ‘noise’. Newton’s primary objectives are to serve clients well and to focus on investment returns.

Capabilities
Equities: Newton offers core global equity strategies, in addition to international (ex-US), high conviction ‘opportunities’ strategies and equity income strategies.
Fixed income: Newton’s fixed-income range of strategies span global fixed income asset classes, including global government bonds, high-yield bonds and an unconstrained, absolute-return global bond strategy.
Multi-asset: Newton’s multi-asset strategies encompass both absolute return and relative-return mandates. Newton has extensive experience in managing portfolios which invest across the broad global investment universe of assets, and which have the freedom to control asset allocation and security selection according to long-term objectives, without adherence to market index constraints.
