Manager Commentary as at 30 September 2022

The Global Equity fund had positive performance over Q3, and marginally outperformed benchmark. At a regional level, an underweight allocation to India detracted from performance. The exposure to quality names within the portfolio was beneficial amid economic uncertainty. At a sector level, fund returns were driven by strong security selection in Health and Industrial sectors, but these gains mitigated by poor performance in the Consumer Discretionary sector, particularly within the auto industry.

At stock level, the top performer of the fund was Biogen Inc, whose share price jumped in September after positive testing results of their new Alzheimer's therapy, Lecanamab. The largest detractors from the fund were electric vehicle companies XPeng Inc, BYD and Li Auto, where investors have shown concern about slowing growth in this sector and supply chain delays.

Assessing Performance

All St. James’s Place funds are designed for the use of the advised clients of St. James’s Place Partners, who will typically recommend a diversified portfolio of funds to meet the needs and objectives of each client. The most appropriate assessment of performance is therefore conducted in conjunction with your Partner, reviewing the performance of your portfolio against the objectives for which it was established.

Due to the nature of the assets held in the fund, we suggest reviewing the performance over a minimum 5-year period to reflect the fact that it is intended to be a medium to long term investment and short term market fluctuations are inevitable. In the assessment of performance, it is important to understand how the fund's return has been achieved, the level of risk taken, or avoided, and the outcomes of the decisions taken by the Fund Manager. The commentary in the half-yearly and annual reports may provide helpful material to support this assessment. Investors can also refer to the MSCI AC World Index as context in assessing the performance of the fund, because it provides an indication of the markets in which the fund invests.
