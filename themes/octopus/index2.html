What is this Product?

The legal form of the product is a Venture Capital Trust (VCT) which is an investment company listed on the London Stock Exchange and set up to invest in small UK businesses that meet certain criteria. The objective of the product is to deliver growth to investors through tax-free growth and tax-free dividends, which is achieved by giving people the opportunity to participate in the growth of some of the UK’s most entrepreneurial high growth businesses. This investment is deemed to be high risk by the FCA and is suitable for UK resident taxpayers who can tolerate higher risk and have a time horizon of greater than five years. Shares of Octopus Titan VCT plc are bought and sold via markets. Typically, at any given time on any given day, the price you pay for a share will be higher than the price at which you could sell it. While there is no recommended holding period for VCT shares they must be held for a minimum of 5 years in order to retain the upfront income tax relief, tax free dividends and tax free growth.

How long should I hold it and can I take money out early?

You can disinvest prior to the 5 year minimum holding period (subject to liquidity) although any tax credit you receive will be claimed back by HMRC as a result.
