What is this product?

Type

The Fund is a Delaware series limited partnership, established under the laws of the State of Delaware.

Term

The Fund will mature and therefore dissolve and terminate at the end of its term. The Fund's term expires on the earliest to occur of (i) one year after the date by which all of the Fund's portfolio investments have been liquidated and the Fund's obligations (including contingent obligations) have been terminated (or reasonable provision has otherwise been made for such obligations), and (ii) a decision by the General Partner, in its sole discretion, to terminate the Fund. The term is therefore not certain and may be longer than ten years, but for illustrative purposes is treated as ten years in this document.

Objectives

The investment objective of the Fund is to seek to achieve attractive total returns principally through a strategy of making investments in businesses managing alternative investment vehicles (Management Companies), which primarily manage alternative investment funds with investment objectives of investing in private investments and other investments that have limited liquidity, such as investments in private equity, real estate, infrastructure and various private debt securities and instruments. However, given the convergence of strategies in the alternatives industry, the Fund also may invest with Management Companies that offer other types of products (either in addition to such alternative investment funds or instead of them), including funds with investment objectives of investing primarily in publicly-traded investments.

The Fund intends to pursue this objective primarily by providing capital to and / or taking minority non-control positions in such Management Companies on a global basis. Potential drivers of return include participation in the management fees as well as carried interest and other performance-based fees received by Management Companies, growth in profitability of Management Companies, and increases in the franchise value of the Management Companies.

The Fund will seek to make investments that provide income to the Fund so that, subject to the receipt of such income, the Fund is able to make distributions to investors from time to time. In connection with the foregoing, the Fund primarily will seek to structure investments in Management Companies such that the Fund will share in income earned by such Management Companies and their key personnel and affiliated entities, including those acting as investment manager, general partner or managing member of underlying investment funds or otherwise, as well as income streams from the managed accounts of such Management Companies, personnel and entities, although no assurance can be given in this regard.

The return of the Fund depends on the performance of the Fund, which is directly related to the performance of its investments. The risk and reward profile of the Fund described in this Key Information Document assumes that you hold your investment in the Fund for at least the Recommended Holding Period as set out below under the heading “How long should I hold it and can I take my money out early?”

As used herein, the term “Goldman Sachs” refers to The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., together with its subsidiaries, including Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and its other subsidiaries and affiliates and the Manufacturer. The Interests are intended only for sophisticated investors for whom an investment in the Fund does not constitute a complete investment program and that fully understand and are willing to assume the risks involved in the Fund's investment program, and can bear the potential loss of their entire investment in the Fund. Each investor must be prepared to bear the economic risk of the investment for an indefinite period because the Interests are subject to sale, transfer and redemption restrictions.

The Fund is offering Interests to eligible Goldman Sachs employees who are (i) U.S. citizens (wherever located), (ii) holders of U.S. green cards or (iii) U.S. resident aliens, as well as to certain other eligible investors. For certain offerees who are not (i) U.S. citizens, (ii) holders of U.S. green cards, or (iii) U.S. resident aliens, as well as for certain other eligible investors, Goldman Sachs is concurrently offering interests in the Offshore Fund, as described under “Summary of Terms of the Fund — The Offshore Fund.” in the Offering Document.

Each investor must qualify as an “accredited investor” under the income test set forth in Rule 501(a)(6) of Regulation D under the U.S. Securities Act of 1993, as amended (the 1933 Act) or, in the case of Affiliated Investor Entities, under another provision of Rule 501(a). By executing the subscription agreement, each Limited Partner will represent as to its eligibility to invest in the Fund pursuant to the foregoing requirements.

How long should I hold it and can I take money out early?

Recommended holding period: until the end of the term of the Fund as described above under the heading “What is this product”. It is expected to be 9 years.

Required minimum holding period: until the end of the term of the Fund. The recommended holding period and the required minimum holding period of the Fund have been selected as the Fund is a closed-ended fund with no right for investors to redeem their investment. The Fund is designed for investors to hold their interest in the Fund until the end of the Fund's term when all underlying investments have been liquidated and proceeds distributed to investors in the Fund.
This Fund is not a product which can be easily sold or transferred. You should assume that as an investor in the Fund, you will not be able to transfer your Interests, directly or indirectly. Withdrawal from the Fund is generally not permitted and investors do not have the right to require redemption of their Interests prior to the winding up of the Fund.
You may incur additional costs, losses or adverse tax consequences if Goldman Sachs determines that (a) you leave Goldman Sachs, for any reason (whether due to voluntary or involuntary termination of employment), before December 31, 2021, (b) you Fail to Consider Risk (as defined in the Offering Document) at any time prior to the liquidation of the Fund, (c) at any time you engage in conduct which constitutes Cause (as defined below) or (d) at any time fail to certify that you are in compliance with, or the General Partner determines that you fail to comply with, all the terms and conditions of the Partnership Agreement (as defined in the Offering Document) and your subscription materials. In such case, the General Partner will have the right, but not the obligation, to convert your Interest to a Class C interest in the Fund which, subject to applicable law, will be subject to management fees and carried interest. “Cause” is defined in the Offering Memorandum and includes, amongst others, conviction in a criminal proceeding on a misdemeanour or felony charge, engaging in conduct detrimental to the Firm and engaging in any conduct which constitutes an employment disqualification.
