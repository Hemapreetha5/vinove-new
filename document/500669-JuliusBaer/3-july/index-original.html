Fund opportunities

This actively managed fund invests in core Japanese large-cap companies that are priced at a discount to their underlying business value. The strategy has achieved consistent performance without deviating largely in terms of sectors or style from its comparison benchmark. The Fund is aimed at investors with a medium-to-long-term time horizon who are looking for actively managed exposure to Japanese equities with a core approach.

Main risk factors

The Fund features a single country exposure to Japanese companies. Hence, its performance is highly dependent on the returns of this underlying market. The Fund is actively managed with a mid-to-long-term investment horizon. In the short term, it may deviate from and underperform its comparison index. For a complete overview of all risks attached to the Fund, please refer to the “Risk Factors” section in the Supplement and the Prospectus.
