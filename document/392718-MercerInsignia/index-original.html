Investment Objective

The Portfolio invests in a mix of income and growth assets, which may include Australian and International Equities, property and infrastructure, alternatives, fixed income and income securities and cash. The portfolio is designed to reduce overall portfolio risk by spreading investments across a number of specialist managers with complementary investment management styles.
