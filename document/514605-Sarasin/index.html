Fund Facts

Fund Aim

The Fund seeks to provide growth (through increases in investment value) over rolling five-year period through investment in the shares of large and medium sized companies from across the world. We identify investment themes that we believe will drive long term global growth and lead to disruption in global economies and industries. The Fund invests in companies which are most likely to benefit from our investment themes and may be from any country/region, sector or industry. The Fund avoids companies which are materially engaged in certain sectors, including tobacco, alcohol, armaments, gambling, adult entertainment and the extraction of fossil fuels, and those that we have judged to exhibit excessive ESG weaknesses.

Investment Strategy

The Fund will carefully select investments and has free choice to select companies from any country/region, sector or industry.

The Fund seeks to identify the long-term investment themes that drive growth and lead to disruption in global economies and industries, and will shape the world in which we live and invest. The Fund will select ethical (socially responsible) companies based on analysis of which are most likely to benefit from our themes, and are well placed to grow their revenues and cash flows as a result of them. The Fund will choose global themes which track long-term worldwide growth trends (rather than those relevant only to particular regions or markets).

The Fund will avoid companies that are materially engaged in certain sectors, including the production or distribution of tobacco, alcohol, armaments, gambling, adult entertainment and the extraction of fossil fuels.
