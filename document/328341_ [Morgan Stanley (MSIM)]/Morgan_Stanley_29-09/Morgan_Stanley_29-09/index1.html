What happens if MSIM Fund Management (Ireland) Limited is unable to pay out?

The assets of the Fund are held in safekeeping by the Depositary. In the event of the insolvency of the Management Company, the Fund's assets in the safekeeping of the Depositary will not be affected. In the event of the Depositary's insolvency, the Fund may suffer a financial loss. However, this risk is mitigated to a certain extent as the Depositary is required by law and regulation to segregate its own assets from those of the Fund. The Depositary will also be liable to the Fund and the investors for any loss arising from its negligence, fraud or intentional failure to fulfill its obligations. Such loss is not covered by an investors compensation or protection scheme.
