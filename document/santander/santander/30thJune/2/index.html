DEFINITIONS

Pay Date: This is the date on which the fund will pay income to eligible investors.

XD Date: This is the date after which new investors who buy shares in the fund will not be eligible to receive the next income payment.

Historic Yield: The sum of income payments announced in the previous 12 months, divided by the fund price as at the date of the factsheet.

Distribution Yield: The amount that may be expected to be distributed over the next 12 months as a percentage of the fund price as at the date of the factsheet. This is based on a snapshot of the portfolio on that day, and not guaranteed.

Underlying Yield: The annualised income (net of expenses) as a percentage of the market value of the fund as at the factsheet date. This is based on a snapshot of the portfolio on that day, and not guaranteed.

Annual Income Delivered: Sum of all income payments made over the fund's last accounting year. Each income payment is a percentage of the fund price as at the previous XD date, with each accounting year ending on 31 March.

Investors may be subject to tax on income payments they receive.

FUND OBJECTIVE AND POLICY

The Fund’s objective is to provide an income, with the potential for capital growth over 5+ years. The Fund has a target annual income of 2.5 - 3.5% (not guaranteed).

The Fund is actively managed and invests globally (up to 15% in non-developed markets): 60-90% in bonds (at least 80% investment grade; 10-30% in shares in listed companies, including REITs and other investment trusts; up to 10% in cash/ cash like instruments; and up to 6% in asset/mortgage backed securities. Up to 15% can be invested in non-developed markets. At least 75% will be in bonds denominated in or hedged to GBP, and shares in companies listed in the UK. The Fund typically invests directly but can invest indirectly: up to 10% via other funds; and in property via REITs. Derivative strategies will be used to help deliver income. They will be used regularly for efficient portfolio management (inc. hedging), and less frequently for investment purposes. The ACD and appointed Sub-Investment Managers each manage a portion of the Fund (Mandates). They will use discretion to select assets according to their investment views, subject to a performance targets relative to the Constraint Benchmark and risk management measures, with focus on income with the aim of meeting the Fund’s objectives. The Constraint Benchmark has been selected as it is representative of the assets the Fund may invest in.
