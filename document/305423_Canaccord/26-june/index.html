Select Portfolio Service Cautious (GBP)

Investment objective

The Select Portfolio Service invests across the Canaccord Genuity Fund Range. The weightings between the funds are managed within closely defined parameters so as to maximise the level of return for a defined level of risk. Using Canaccord’s sophisticated modelling systems, the underlying funds have been blended to ensure that the best outcome is achieved for investors along the efficient frontier, and an on-going portfolio optimisation process ensures that the fund managers are able to take advantage of prevailing market conditions in order to maximise returns while maintaining the level of target volatility. The risk based approach to portfolio construction combined with Canaccord’s rigorous investment management processes deliver a range of actively managed solutions that are tightly aligned to clients’ expectations.
