Fund objective

The fund aims to provide a return in-line with its benchmark by investing in a portfolio of balanced open-ended UK property funds. The fund is actively managed, so returns may not replicate those of the benchmark. The consideration of Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) issues is integrated into the investment process of this fund.
