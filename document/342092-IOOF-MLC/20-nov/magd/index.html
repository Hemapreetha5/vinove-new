Investment strategy

The portfolio focused on long-term capital growth, generally borrows to invest in growth assets and comfortable with the extra volatility associated with gearing.

Quarter highlights

Key contributors to performance for the quarter ended 30 September 2023 are:

  • In another volatile quarter, there were negative returns in share markets and all maturity bonds. The exposure to private equity (+3.0%) helped cushion the negative returns.

Performance relative to benchmark

The portfolio has achieved its return objective to provide a return higher than its benchmark (before fees) over 5 year periods, due to our overweight to unhedged global shares and active management of Australian and global shares. Over the 5 years to 30 September 2023, the portfolio has produced a return of 8.8% pa which is 0.6% pa above its benchmark (before fees).
