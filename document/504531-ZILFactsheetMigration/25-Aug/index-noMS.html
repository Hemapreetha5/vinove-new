Ravenscroft Global Income

Fund Objective

The Global Income Fund’s objective is to provide investors with an income yield that competes with prevailing cash rates while delivering some capital growth. This income stream can be re-invested to provide a cautious approach to capital growth. The fund invests in a global portfolio of bonds and dividend paying equities with an emphasis on quality and value. Ravenscroft Global Income is a sub-fund of the Ravenscroft Investment Fund Offshore; an open-ended, multi-class Unit Trust authorised as a Class B Scheme by the Guernsey Financial Services Commission.
