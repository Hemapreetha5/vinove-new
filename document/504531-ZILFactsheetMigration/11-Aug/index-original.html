Fund Objective

The Zurich Climate Focus World Equity Fund (the ‘Fund’) aims to track the return performance of the MSCI World Climate Paris Aligned Ex Select Business Involvement Screens Index (the ‘Index’) before fees and expenses. The Fund is passively managed.
